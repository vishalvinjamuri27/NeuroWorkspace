# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for contributors of
all backgrounds and experience levels. Be constructive, assume good intent while
addressing concrete impact, respect privacy and scientific uncertainty, and avoid
demeaning or discriminatory conduct.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that threatens safety, privacy,
collaboration, or project integrity. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer contact published for the repository. Enforcement decisions should be
documented privately, proportionate, and free of retaliation.
